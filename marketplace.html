FAQ

What is this marketplace ?

A notice board for used Cybercabs, between owners . You list a car, or you tell us you are looking for one . We introduce buyer and seller .

Who can list ?

Anyone can list . You do not have to be a community member, though joining Tesla Cybercab Fleet Owners on X is the easiest way to follow introductions and new listings .

Can I list a car before Tesla has consented to the sale ?

Yes . Listings may appear as Tesla consent pending . Resale still needs Tesla's written consent, and an unapproved sale can be void . We will not introduce parties who plan to skip that consent .

Can a listing include the fleet assignment or Robotaxi authorization ?

Yes, where we know it . A listing can describe the car's current Tesla authorization . Transfer of that authorization depends on Tesla, and it does not travel with the car unless Tesla confirms that in writing .

Do you handle the payment, the title or escrow ?

No . We only introduce buyer and seller . After that, they handle Tesla's consent, the Tesla app ownership transfer, title, transport and payment themselves .

When will listings appear ?

First deliveries of personal Cybercabs are only just beginning . This page starts as a waitlist . Approved listings will appear here once owners have cars to sell .

How do you use my details ?

Your form goes to our community inbox . We use it only to follow up on your listing or search, and to make an introduction after we approve it . We do not publish your VIN, email or home address on this page .

Is this affiliated with Tesla ?

No . Cybercab Fleet Owners is an independent community, not affiliated with Tesla, Inc. Tesla's own agreements control resale, software and operating area .

Is there a fee ?

The board is free, and so is the community . We do not take a deposit or a commission on a sale .

The community stays free . Join Tesla Cybercab Fleet Owners on X .
