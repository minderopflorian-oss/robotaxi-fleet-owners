Megapod as a Service

A power and cooling pod for a robotaxi fleet hub or a small AI compute site . Storage , liquid cooling and load-balancing software in one unit , installed , monitored and tuned for you instead of bought once and left alone .

A community concept · not a Tesla product · not for sale
Join the Megapod interest listHow it would work
The problem

Power is the bottleneck

A robotaxi hub needs a lot of power at the same moments every day . Cars come back together , charge together and need to be ready together . The grid connection is often smaller than the peak , and in places like the Netherlands a bigger one can take years .

PEAKS

Depot charging peaks

Many cars plug in at once after the morning and evening rush . The peak decides the size of the grid connection you need .

GRID

Grid limits

Congested grids mean long waits or no new capacity at all . A hub that can't charge can't put cars on the road .

BACKUP

No power , no fleet

An outage at the depot stops charging , cleaning and dispatch . A fleet hub needs a plan for backup power .

HEAT

Heat and cooling

Fast chargers , batteries and any on-site compute all produce heat . Cooling is often an afterthought until it fails .

COMPUTE

AI compute wants power too

Demand for AI compute is growing fast , and compute sites face the same grid and cooling limits as a depot .

SKILLS

Energy is a specialist job

Most fleet owners want to run cars , not become an energy company . Sizing , permits and tuning take know-how .

What a Megapod is

One pod : store , cool , balance

The idea is a factory-built unit that sits at the edge of a fleet hub or next to a small compute room . It combines three things that are usually bought and run separately .

01 · STORE

Integrated battery storage

Megapack-style storage that fills up slowly from the grid or solar and gives power back fast when the cars arrive . It also covers short outages .

02 · COOL

Liquid cooling

Liquid cooling of the kind used in electric cars , sized for batteries , chargers and racks of compute in the same pod .

03 · BALANCE

Load-balancing software

On-site software that decides second by second where power goes : cars first , then storage , then compute , within the limit of the grid connection .

"Megapod" is our working name for this concept . Tesla has filed a MEGAPOD trademark for modular AI data-center hardware , but has not announced any product or service like this . What is known about the Megapod

How the service works

As a service , not a one-off purchase

Instead of buying hardware and figuring it out alone , the hub gets the pod plus the people and software that keep it right-sized over time .

STEP 1

Site check

Look at the grid connection , the number of cars , the charging pattern and any compute plans . Size the pod to the real peaks .

STEP 2

Install

Deliver and connect a factory-built pod , with permits , fire safety and the link to the chargers handled by a partner .

STEP 3

Monitor

Watch storage , cooling and charging around the clock . Spot problems before a car misses a shift .

STEP 4

Optimise

Keep tuning the software as the fleet grows : new cars , new shifts , new compute . Add pods when the hub outgrows one .

Who it's for

Built around the fleet hub

A single car charging at home doesn't need a pod . This concept is for places where many cars , or cars and compute , share one connection .

Fleet hub and depot operators

Hubs that charge , clean and park robotaxis and keep running into the limits of their grid connection .

Growing fleet owners

Owners planning to go from a few cars to a hub of their own , who want power sorted before the cars arrive .

Small AI compute sites

Edge compute rooms that need steady power , cooling and backup , possibly sharing a site with a depot .

Site owners and partners

Parking garages , landlords , installers and energy partners who want to host or build hubs together with the community .

FAQ

Questions

Is Megapod as a Service a Tesla product ?

No . It's a concept discussed in our independent community . Tesla has not announced anything like it , and this site is not affiliated with Tesla, Inc.

Can I buy or order one ?

No . Nothing is for sale . The interest list helps us learn which hubs and sites would want this , so we know what to explore with partners .

What would it cost ?

There is no pricing , and we don't publish price or earnings estimates . Any real offer would come from a licensed provider with its own terms .

Do I need a pod for one or two cars ?

Probably not . Home or small-depot charging is usually enough . The concept starts to matter when many cars share one grid connection .

How does it help with grid congestion ?

Storage fills up slowly over the day and gives power back during charging peaks , so the hub can charge more cars than the grid connection alone would allow .

Where did the idea come from ?

It builds on an idea raised on X about power modules that combine storage , liquid cooling and load-balancing software . We applied it to fleet hubs .
