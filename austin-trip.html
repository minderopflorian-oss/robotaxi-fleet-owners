Fleet Owners Trip : Austin

We are going to Austin , Texas , together with members of the community . To see the Robotaxi network in real life , learn from the people who run cars there today , and meet each other in person .

Planned for 2027 . The date will be announced together with members .

Why Austin ?

The first Robotaxi city

Tesla started its Robotaxi service in Austin on June 22 , 2025 . In September 2026 it began offering rides in limited areas of Austin in the two-seat Cybercab .

Sources : Reuters , June 2025 · Reuters , Sept 2026

Where the Cybercab is built

Cybercab production started at Gigafactory Texas in Austin in April 2026 . Tesla's Q3 2026 update lists Cybercab in Texas as in production .

Sources : Electrek · Tesla Q3 2026 update

What we plan

See Robotaxis in action

Ride along and watch how pickups , drop-offs and service areas work on the street .

Talk to early operators

Hear what running cars on a live network takes , from cleaning to uptime .

Depot and charging look

Look at what a depot and charging site needs : space , power and logistics .

Meet members

Meet fellow future fleet owners and our expert team , and compare your 90-Day Fleet Plan .

Costs are paid by each participant . Details on the program , travel and stay follow when the date is set . This is an independent community trip , not organised by or affiliated with Tesla , Inc . Visits depend on what is open to the public at that time .
