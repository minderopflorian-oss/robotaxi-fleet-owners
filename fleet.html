Fleet owner FAQ

What we know.

How do I own a Cybercab fleet?

Start with a plan: pick your city, decide on fleet size, and work out depot, charging, cleaning, insurance and funding. Tesla said customer cars should start joining the robotaxi network around Q2 2027. The free 2027 Readiness Guide walks through each step, and members compare plans in the X community.

When can private owners add cars to the network?

On Tesla's Q3 2026 earnings call, Tesla said customer cars should start joining the robotaxi fleet around Q2 2027. Timelines are Tesla's guidance and can change.

When can I buy a Cybercab?

Tesla said Cybercab deliveries to customers start by the end of 2026. Elon Musk also said the vast majority of Cybercabs will be Tesla-owned, so expect small private numbers at first.

Is this community run by Tesla?

No. It's an independent community of owners and enthusiasts, not affiliated with Tesla, Inc. Some people search for it as "Cybercap fleet owners": it's the same community.

How do I get the guides?

Request the free 2027 Readiness Guide above, or ask for one of the full guides by email. Anyone planning or curious about a robotaxi fleet is welcome, wherever you live, and you do not need to own a Tesla to join the community.
